Data sources

Plan&Ride uses these open data. Their licences ask us to name the source, so we name them here, in the app (Settings → Data sources) and in our source code.

Banská Bystrica Self-Governing Region, open data

The stops of the IDS BBSK integrated transport system with their fare zone and position, and the IDS BBSK fare zones, from the BBSK open data portal. We use them to work out the price of a journey.

Licences: stops CC BY-SA, zones CC BY 4.0. Our file of stops with their zones (ids_bbsk_stops.json) is shared under the same CC BY-SA licence.

City of Bratislava, open data

Bratislava public transport timetables (Dopravný podnik Bratislava) from the Bratislava open data portal, licensed CC BY 4.0.

gtfs.sk

City bus timetables of Pezinok, Malacky and Topoľčany (AZ-Lines) from the open data collection gtfs.sk, licensed CC BY 4.0. From its corrected copy of the ŽSR timetable we also take the trains' routes along the tracks, which gtfs.sk traced from OpenStreetMap.

Železnice Slovenskej republiky

The train timetable from the national open data catalogue, licensed CC BY 1.0.

OpenStreetMap

Maps and walking routes: © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL).

Open-Meteo

Weather when you search for connections: Open-Meteo.com, licensed CC BY 4.0.
